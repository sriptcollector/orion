    about these readings

    These signals are computed from MediaPipe pose and face-blendshape landmarks. They are heuristics — useful for surfacing patterns, not for diagnosing what someone "really" thinks. Body language is context-dependent. Treat the readout as a magnifying glass, not a verdict.
